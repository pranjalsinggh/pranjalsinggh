# Hi, I'm Pranjal

I build AI-powered tools that turn conversations and research into useful insights. My projects bring together data analysis, evidence-backed answers, and practical web interfaces.

## Selected work

### [ChatScope](https://github.com/pranjalsinggh/chatscope)

Turn WhatsApp exports into interactive analytics and grounded AI answers. Includes conversation trends, message search, and Q&A in English, Hindi, and Hinglish.

`React` `TypeScript` `Node.js` `Express` `Gemini`

[Explore the code](https://github.com/pranjalsinggh/chatscope) · [Try the app](https://chatscope-oq9u.onrender.com/)

### [ScholarScout](https://github.com/pranjalsinggh/ScholarScout)

Explore research PDFs with source-linked analysis and evidence search. Combines local retrieval with optional AI synthesis and related-paper discovery.

`Python` `FastAPI` `React` `TypeScript` `scikit-learn`

[Explore the code](https://github.com/pranjalsinggh/ScholarScout) · [Try the app](https://scholarscout-dskr.onrender.com/)
